A lineup is not a list. It is a handful of rooms.

Pick twenty films one at a time and you can still end up reaching the same audience five times over. Slate reads the set as a whole and shows which items reach the same people, which one reaches people nothing else does, and whether anything connects them.

It works on anything Qloo can rank. Tested on films, music, books, podcasts and TV shows.

Film festivals

Here is what Slate sees in a lineup, using three real film festivals: Berlinale 2024 in Germany, Cannes 2023 in France and Sundance 2024 in the United States.

Bridge

A film that reaches two different crowds at once. It is the overlap between them, so someone who came for one group finds something next door. Instead of programming two separate nights, one slot covers both.

Outlier

A film that covers an audience with a distinct taste from the rest of the lineup. If it sits in a group, it is the one most weakly tied to it: it covers some of that group’s audience, and some that nothing else in the lineup covers.

Slate can also propose the change

It takes one of the slots the lineup can spare and adds something that reaches a room the lineup is missing. Every candidate is tested against the whole set, which is rebuilt from scratch each time, so the groups are worked out again rather than one slot changing. A change is kept only if the measured shape improves. Finding nothing is an allowed answer.
